## Core Expectations
* **Professional Behavior:** Treat all speakers, attendees, volunteers, and staff with respect and kindness.
* **Inclusive Environment:** Harassment, discrimination, or exclusionary remarks based on gender, sexual orientation, race, religion, or disability are strictly prohibited.
* **Appropriate Content:** Presentations, online chats, and social media posts must remain appropriate for a diverse, professional audience without offensive or sexualized imagery/language.

## Media and Communications
* **Recording Notice:** Attendees may be photographed or recorded during sessions; individuals with concerns should notify event organizers directly.
* **Mailing Lists:** Sign-ups tied to event registration include clear, accessible opt-out and unsubscribe mechanisms.

## Enforcement
* **Violations:** Organizers reserve the right to remove participants or revoke access to sessions or platforms without a refund for behavior breaching community safety and respect.

If you need to report an incident or require details for a specific event hosted during the festival, please let me know the name of the specific event or organization so I can provide precise contact details.
