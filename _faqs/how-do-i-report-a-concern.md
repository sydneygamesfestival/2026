---
title: What if I have a safety or conduct concern at an event?
order: 100
featured: false
---

Tell the relevant event organiser using the reporting process for that event. If contacting the organiser would be unsafe or inappropriate, or the response has been inadequate, [contact the festival team]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}). See the [Code of Conduct]({{ '/code-of-conduct/' | relative_url }}) for the full reporting process. If someone is in immediate danger, contact venue staff or security and call **000**.
