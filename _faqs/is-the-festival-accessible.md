title: Is the festival accessible?
order: 50
featured: true
---

Events take place at different venues and are run by different organisers, so access arrangements vary. Check the organiser's event details through the [program]({{ '/program/' | relative_url }}) for venue information and any accessibility details. If that information is missing, ask the organiser or [contact the festival team]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}). Our [Code of Conduct]({{ '/code-of-conduct/' | relative_url }}) asks organisers to share accessibility information and provide a way to request reasonable adjustments.
