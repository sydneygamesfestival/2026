title: Do I need a ticket, and are events free?

Booking and prices are set by each event organiser. In the [program]({{ '/program/' | relative_url }}), use an event's **Event details** link to check whether you need to register or buy a ticket, and what it costs. If a listing says **Details TBA**, check back as the organiser confirms its plans.
